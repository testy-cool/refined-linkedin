Refined LinkedIn

To fold a job description, Refined LinkedIn sends its text to the AI service you choose below, once per job. To compare a job with your CV, it sends the description with what you write under About you. Nothing else leaves your browser: your notes, highlight words and the jobs you saw stay in Chrome on this computer.

About you

Paste your CV, and add anything else about you: what you have done that the CV leaves out, what you want, and what you don't want. Under an open job, Compare with my CV underlines its bullet points: green where this shows you have it, yellow where it doesn't, red where you said you don't want it.

PDF, Word (.docx) or text, or drop the file on the box. Its text replaces what is in the box. The file stays on this computer.

Folding prompt

What the model is asked for each job. {TEXT} is where the job description goes. The answer always comes back in the same format, a list of passages to fold, so only the instructions change.

Your jobs

Refined LinkedIn keeps every job you open or scroll past in a list, on this computer: its description, title, employer and place, when you opened it, and your notes. Download them all as one file, which an AI chat or a script can read.
